CLIP STUDIO PRO v1 — NEW FEATURES REMAINING
(Brainstorm list. Copied exactly as told to the user on 2026-07-02.)

===================================================================

Here's what Pro v1 does today, so we're building on reality: YouTube-segment
or local-file -> vertical 1080x1920 Short, with headline (emoji-capable),
karaoke captions (whisper), channel watermark, source credit, subscribe outro,
AI intro-hook voiceover (Piper), AI auto-zoom (Gemini-picked moments), and
-14 LUFS loudness normalization. Gemini and youtube-transcript are already
wired in, and it stores the transcript per clip.

Given that, here are the highest-leverage additions, grouped by theme. I've
flagged effort and whether it reuses infra you already have — important because
the phone OOMs at the whisper stage, so I'm steering away from anything that
adds heavy compute.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
TOP PICKS (reuse Gemini/transcript you already have — low/medium effort)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Auto-clip finder — paste a full video URL, Gemini reads the transcript and
   suggests 3-5 viral-worthy segments with start/end + a headline each. This
   removes the most manual part of your workflow (hunting timestamps). Biggest
   payoff.

2. Auto-headline — generate the headline from the clip's transcript instead of
   typing it. You already fetch the transcript and have Gemini.

3. Auto voiceover-hook — right now you type/paste the hook from the Gemini app
   manually. Generate it in-app from the clip context. Closes that loop.

4. Auto title + description + hashtags — Gemini writes the upload metadata,
   shown in the UI / saved as a sidecar .txt next to the clip.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
FORMAT & REACH (medium effort, mostly ffmpeg)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Platform presets — add 1:1 (Instagram feed) and 16:9 alongside the current
   9:16, so one clip serves Shorts + Reels + TikTok + feed.

6. Batch queue from one video — accept several segments at once (pairs
   perfectly with #1) and render them in sequence.

7. Caption style presets — a couple of switchable karaoke looks (e.g. a bold
   "Hormozi" style) rather than the single current style.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
POLISH (ffmpeg, low/medium effort)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
8. Background music with auto-ducking — royalty-free bed under the speech,
   ducked when there's dialogue (you already do LUFS work, so the audio graph
   is familiar).

9. Progress bar strip at the bottom of the frame (a very common Shorts
   retention trick).

10. Auto thumbnail — pull a strong frame + the headline into a thumbnail image.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
HEAVIER — flagging the cost given the phone
-------------------------------------------------------------------
11. Silence removal / auto jump-cuts — trims dead air in talking-head clips.
    Big quality win but adds an analysis pass.

12. Face-tracking reframe — keep the speaker centered in the vertical crop
    instead of blurred bars. Most "pro" upgrade, but it's the most
    compute-hungry and risky on the phone (OOM territory) — I'd only attempt
    this after moving renders off the phone.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
RECOMMENDATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Start with #1 Auto-clip finder (or the #1-#4 "AI assist" bundle) — it's the
highest workflow impact and reuses infra you already have, so it's low-risk on
the phone.
